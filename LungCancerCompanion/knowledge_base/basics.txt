What is lung cancer?
Lung cancer happens when cells in the lungs grow in an uncontrolled way and form a tumor. It can affect how well you breathe and can spread (metastasize) to other parts of the body over time.

Types of lung cancer
There are two main types of lung cancer:
- Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)
- Small cell lung cancer (SCLC)

Non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC)
NSCLC is the most common type of lung cancer. It makes up about 80–85% of lung cancer cases.
Common subtypes of NSCLC include:
- Adenocarcinoma
- Squamous cell carcinoma
- Large cell carcinoma

Small cell lung cancer (SCLC)
Small cell lung cancer is less common, making up about 10–15% of lung cancer cases.
SCLC tends to grow and spread more quickly than NSCLC, and it is often treated with chemotherapy, radiation, or a combination of treatments.
Knowing whether someone has NSCLC or SCLC helps doctors choose the most appropriate tests and treatment options. It also helps in understanding prognosis and planning follow-up care.
